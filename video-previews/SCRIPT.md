# Portfolio Reels — Silent Screen Script

These loops are embedded inside portfolio cards and intentionally contain no narration, captions or sound. The visual script replaces spoken lines with two real captured states per project.

## GrooMyCRM — 6.5 seconds

1. Open on the hero: “Салон растёт. Хвосты виляют.”
2. Travel toward the CRM calendar: “Неделя без накладок.”
3. Return to the peach hero through a lime-edged wipe.

## TrimmyCRM — 6.5 seconds

1. Open inside the 3D salon with “Салон. Включён.”
2. Push slowly into the salon world and emphasize the chair corridor.
3. Close through a deep-green iris that loops back to the opening frame.

## 24Uptime — 6.5 seconds

1. Open on the monitoring world map and glass telemetry panels.
2. Reveal the real analytics view with response and weekly-check charts.
3. Sweep back to the hero using a blue signal line.

## Bankrot.AI — 6.5 seconds

1. Open on the product hero and the line “Там, где заканчиваются шаблоны”.
2. Reveal the working product cards against the green digital contour.
3. Return to the hero through a dark radial mask.

## Lex-Doc Sorter — 6.5 seconds

1. Open on “Lex-Doc Sorter” and the moving document stack.
2. Reveal the large “Документы под контролем” state.
3. Return through a narrow electric-blue wipe.
